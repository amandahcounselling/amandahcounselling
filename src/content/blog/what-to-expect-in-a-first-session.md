Starting counselling can feel like a big step, especially if you are not sure what will happen in the first appointment. A first session is usually a gentle beginning rather than a test or interview you need to prepare for perfectly.

Your counsellor may ask what brings you in, what you hope to get from counselling, and what support has or has not helped before. You can share at your own pace. You are also welcome to ask questions about confidentiality, fees, scheduling, and the counsellor's approach.

By the end of the first session, you should have a clearer sense of whether the fit feels right and what next steps might look like.
