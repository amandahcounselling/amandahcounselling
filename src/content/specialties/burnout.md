**_Do any of these sound familiar_?**

• You **_chronically over-commit_**, taking on extra shifts at work or saying "yes" to all of the invitations (fear of missing out/FOMO is so real)

• You want to **_rest_**, but when you do get the chance, it feels unfamiliar (maybe even uncomfortable), so you keep filling your schedule to the brim

• You're the one who **_supports everyone_** at the drop of a hat, including that person you met 5 minutes ago, but you don't ask or get the same in return

• You have **_high expectations_** of yourself, and you think you should be able to complete everything on your to-do list

• You've been **_juggling_** raising kids, taking care of aging parents, trying to advance your career, and/or working to make enough money to survive in this economy


**____________________________________________________________________________________**


Whatever your reason may be, burnout is a real experience that effects so many people. While burnout and depression may seem similar on the surface, **_burnout tends to be in response to an immediate and/or long-standing stressor in a specific area of our lives_**. We may still find joy in our hobbies and in other aspects of life, and once the stressor is removed or dealt with, the feelings of numbness and overwhelm tend to improve. This isn't often the case when it comes to depression, as it tends to be more deeply rooted in our belief systems, which impact our thoughts, feelings, and behaviours in most areas of our lives.

While burnout can be an unpleasant experience, it can also **_serve as a signal that something isn't working for us_**. The numbness, exhaustion, overwhelm, and brain fog can make our lives feel unbearable. But if we turn towards them and explore their potential causes, we may learn a great deal about ourselves and what we need to move forward. Our work together can **_explore the "why" behind the burnout_**. For some, the "why" may be a career that doesn't feel aligned, high-expectations of self, lack of social support, hyper-independence, or a challenging economy. Once we determine what the cause of your burnout is, we can work towards possible solutions that feel feasible for you.
