While burnout is not a pleasant experience, it can serve as a bit of a smoke signal to areas of our life that may not be working for us
Maybe we over-give or over-commit to things, and burnout is our body's way of trying to tell us to slow down
Maybe you're the one who takes on other people's shifts at work or works over-time to be a team player
Maybe slowing down feels unfamiliar, and likely uncomfortable, so you fill your plate to the brim
Maybe you're always the one who supports others and says yes, but never ask for the same in return
Maybe you're juggling parenting, caring for aging parents, making enough money to survive in this economy, while keeping a safe roof over everyone's heads
Maybe there are too many tasks to complete in a day; maybe you have high expectations for yourself that exceed your actual capacity/energy levels
