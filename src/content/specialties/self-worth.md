I don't believe we lose or gain our self-worth. It's not something we have taken away from us due to our behaviour or a situation that happened that we had no control over. I believe we all have inherent value, or worth, but we may be disconnected from it for a variety of reasons. 

Some signs that we may struggle with self-worth or may be disconnected from it include:

• Placing boundaries or sharing an opinion feels terrifying
• Constantly doubting yourself and your choices, both the big ones and small ones
• Striving for a level of perfection in most things you do that consumes you
• Feeling like you don't fit in or no one cares about you, so you avoid social situations
• Doing whatever you need to do to "keep the peace" in your relationships, like saying yes to everything or changing your values/personality/needs
• Having a difficult time accepting compliments, kindness, or praise from others, and you have a difficult time giving these things to yourself
• Over-apologizing or apologizing when it isn't necessary and/or something wasn't your fault
• Being unsure of what your likes, dislikes, desires, needs, boundaries, goals etc. are

While these things may not cause challenges when they happen once in a while (we're all human after all), if we notice that we have a consistent or chronic pattern of experiences like these, it may signal a disconnection from our inherent worth. Being disconnected from our self-worth can impact every facet of our life, and it can lead to things like burnout, depression, or anxiety. 

When we do this work together, we'll look at the different ways you may disconnect from your worth, like the examples listed above or other ones you have noticed for yourself. We'll discuss the things that get in the way of you connecting to it. For example, you may hold beliefs that create feelings of unworthiness or you may have fears around what would happen if you stepped into your worth by placing a boundary or not rooting your worth in perfectionism. 

When we reconnect to our inherent value and worth, we stop allowing other people to be in control of it and begin to trust ourselves to lead our own lives. Life will still present us with challenges, but we experience more moments of contentment, flow, or stability. As we take our values, needs, desires, and goals into consideration, you may notice that it feel easier to navigate the ups and downs of life when you're connected to your more self-worth.
