You have this sneaking suspicion that your challenges may be related to how you see and view yourself, where you don't consider your own needs...

I don't believe we lose or gain self-worth - I believe we all have it within us, but our experiences can disconnect us from it. 
When we do this work, we may look at the ways we disconnect from our worth (avoiding setting boundaries, saying yes when we want to say no), the things that get in the way of us connecting to it (beliefs, past experiences). While these things may not be inherently cause challenges when they happen once in a while (we're all human after all), if we have a consistent pattern of this, it can make us feel depleted and disconnected from ourselves
