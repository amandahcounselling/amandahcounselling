I don't believe we lose or gain self-worth - I believe we all have it within us, but our experiences can disconnect us from it. 
When we do this work, we may look at the ways we disconnect from our worth (avoiding setting boundaries, saying yes when we want to say no), the things that get in the way of us connecting to it (beliefs, past experiences).
