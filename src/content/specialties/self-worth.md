**_I don't believe we lose or gain our self-worth_**. It's not something we have taken away from us due to our behaviour or a situation that happened that we had no control over. I believe we all have inherent value, or worth, but we may be disconnected from it for a variety of reasons. 

**__________________________________________________________________________**

**Signs we may struggle with self-worth or be disconnected from it include:**

• Placing boundaries or sharing an opinion feels **_terrifying_**

• **_Constantly doubting yourself_** and your choices, the big and small ones

• Striving for a level of **_perfection_** in most things you do that is hard to meet

• Feeling like you don't fit in or no one cares about you, so you **_avoid social situations_**

• Doing whatever you need to do to **_"keep the peace"_** in your relationships, like saying yes to everything or changing your values/personality/needs

• Having a **_difficult time accepting compliments_**, **_kindness_**, or **_praise_** from others, and you have a difficult time giving these things to yourself

• **_Over-apologizing_**, apologizing when nothing happened, or apologizing when something wasn't your fault

• Being **_unsure of who you are_**: your likes, dislikes, desires, needs, boundaries, goals etc.

**__________________________________________________________________________**

While these things may not cause challenges when they happen once in a while (we're all human after all), if we notice a **_consistent pattern_** of experiences like these, it may signal a disconnection from our inherent worth. Being disconnected from our self-worth can **_impact every facet of our life_**, and it can lead to things like **_burnout_**, **_depression_**, or **_anxiety_**. 

When we do this work together, we'll look at the different ways you may disconnect from your worth, like the examples listed above or other ones you have noticed for yourself. We'll discuss the things that get in the way of you connecting to it. For example, you may hold **_beliefs_** that create feelings of unworthiness or you may have **_fears_** around what would happen if you stepped into your worth by placing a boundary or not rooting your worth in perfectionism. 

When we reconnect to our inherent value and worth, we stop allowing other people to be in control of it and begin to **_trust ourselves to lead our own lives_**. Life will still present us with challenges, but we experience more moments of contentment, flow, or stability. As we take our values, needs, desires, and goals into consideration, you may notice that it feels **_easier to navigate the ups and downs_** of life when you're **_connected to your self-worth_**.
