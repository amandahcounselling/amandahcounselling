Life transitions are constants in our lives, but that doesn't make navigating them any easier. Sometimes they are ones we intentionally choose, like moving to a new city, and other times they are ones that have been sprung on us, like the unexpected loss of a loved one. They come in different shapes and sizes. Maybe the transition period is brief, where you just need to take a 3 month course to upgrade your skills and keep working in the same field. Or maybe it's a period that will last years, like a getting an undergraduate degree for a complete career change.

Regardless of how it has shown up for you, transitions often shift our identities and roles in life. We may go from being a student to a working professional; we may have lived with roommates, but now we're living with our partner; or, we may end our time as an employee and retire. This can bring up grief, anxiety, numbness, fear, and excitement, all of which can feel difficult to hold on your own at times.

Working with life transitions can involve different steps, depending on where you are:
You may be contemplating a life transition and seeking support to help prepare you for it, plan for it, or mull over whether it's the right choice for you
You may be in the middle of a transition, feeling overwhelmed, confused, anxious, or sad, and need space to process your experience

In our work together, we can discuss a transition you are contemplating to help determine whether it's the right choice for you, prepare you for an anticipated transition by looking at ways to support you through it, or explore the different feelings and experiences a current transition is bringing up.
