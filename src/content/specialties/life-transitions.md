Life transitions are **_constants in our lives_**, but that doesn't make **_navigating them any easier_**. 

Sometimes they are ones we **_intentionally choose_**, like moving to a new city, and other times they are ones that have been **_sprung on us_**, like the unexpected loss of a loved one. Sometimes they involve **_events occurring_** that change our futures, like getting into that program you have been working toward for years; other times, transitions can be ones where an **_event doesn't occur_** that changes the trajectory of our lives. You might not get into that program, and the future you'd started planning and role of "learner" you wanted to adopt don't come to fruition.

<h2 align="center">
  
Regardless of how it has shown up for you, **transitions often shift our identities and roles in life**

</h2>

<p></p>
<p></p>

Because of these shifts, transitions can bring up **_grief_**, **_anxiety_**, **_numbness_**, **_fear_**, **_excitement_**, and **_confusion_**. You may have anticipated some of these feelings arising, like feeling excited and anxious about moving to a new city. But there may have been experiences you didn't anticipate, like you weren't expecting to feel sad 6 months after moving, even though you feel really happy in your new home.

You may also find yourself feeling confused throughout this process. As your roles and identity shift with the transition, you may feel **_unsure of who you are and who you are becoming_**. You may find yourself turning to old coping mechanisms to bring yourself comfort. You may find that you're behaving in ways you didn't before, and you're unsure of whether this is "you" or if it's your system's way of trying to help you survive through a period of change. All of these experiences can feel **_difficult to hold on your own and navigate at times_**.

In our work together, we can discuss a transition you are contemplating to explore whether it may align with you, prepare you for an anticipated transition by looking at ways to support you through it, or explore the different feelings and experiences a current transition is bringing up. We may look at your **_values and needs_** to help guide you through these changes, discuss your **_past experiences with life transitions_**, or **_process the emotions_** that have been stirred up through this shift.

Beginning, middle, or near the end, you can seek help at any time through a life transition to get the support you need.
