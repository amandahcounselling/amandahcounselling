Relationships can bring both comfort and strain. Individual counselling can help you notice repeated patterns, understand emotional reactions, and practise clearer communication and boundaries.

This work may focus on family dynamics, dating, partnership, friendship, workplace relationships, or the way earlier experiences shape current connection. The aim is to support more honesty, self-respect, and care in how you relate to yourself and others.
