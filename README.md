# Amanda Hecimovic Counselling

This is the GitHub repository for my counselling practice website: [amandahcounselling.com](https://amandahcounselling.com).

I’m Amanda (she/her), a Registered Clinical Counsellor (RCC #29097) with the BC Association of Clinical Counsellors, based in Vancouver, BC. I offer a steady place to make sense of what you are carrying. My work focuses on eating disorder recovery and disordered eating — including anorexia, bulimia, orthorexia, binge eating, and body image concerns — and I also welcome clients seeking support with anxiety, depression, life transitions, self-worth, and burnout.
